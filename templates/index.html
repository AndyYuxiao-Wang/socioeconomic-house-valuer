How this works

  • A Random Forest model, trained on around 17,000 English property sales from HM Land Registry, matched to floor area from EPC records.
  • Each sale is joined to its LSOA's socioeconomic profile — local income, school attainment (Attainment 8), employment, health and crime deprivation from the English Indices of Deprivation.
  • The predicted price and its ±10% range come from that model given the property type, floor area and the LSOA's own socioeconomic figures — not from comparable recent sales directly.
